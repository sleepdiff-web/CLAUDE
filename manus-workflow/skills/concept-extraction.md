# Skill spec: `/concept-extraction`

> This is the SPEC to hand Manus when you build the skill — the structure to pour your WAP
> prompts 1–2 into. It is not the WAP prompts themselves.

**Trigger:** `/concept-extraction`
**Input:** a reference-ad transcript (pasted)
**Runs:** WAP prompts 1–2
**Uses context:** `product-context`, `customer-voc`, `master-research`, `ad-references`, `winning-scripts`

## What it must do
1. **Break down the reference ad** — identify its structure and why it works (hook, agitation,
   mechanism reveal, proof, offer). Beat-map it.
2. **Define our structure** — map that structure onto *our* product/mechanism/VOC for the
   current project's buyer problem. Decide positioning and flow.
3. **Write the first script draft** — full draft in our customer's language, pulling VOC and
   the correct mechanism for the chosen angle.

## Output contract
- A short structure/beat map (so you can sanity-check positioning), then
- The full first-draft script.
- No production tags yet (that's `/notion-push`).

## Build note
Feed Manus your WAP Google Doc, tell it "prompts 1–2 become this skill," test on a real
transcript, and correct it with *"learn from this and update the skill"* until it holds.
