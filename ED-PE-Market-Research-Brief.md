# ED & PE / Sexual Performance Anxiety — Deep Market Research Brief
**Audience:** US/UK men suffering from erectile dysfunction (ED) and premature ejaculation (PE) / performance anxiety
**Purpose:** Direct-response marketing angle development — built on clinical epidemiology + voice-of-customer
**Date:** June 2026

> **Sourcing note:** Clinical/epidemiological figures are from peer-reviewed literature (PubMed) and named market sources. Verbatim customer quotes marked **[VERBATIM]** are exact words pulled from peer-reviewed qualitative studies (focus groups/interviews transcribed word-for-word) or named first-person features. Items marked **[indexed/paraphrase]** were surfaced from Reddit/Trustpilot/Amazon via search extraction (those platforms block automated page-fetching) — reliable in substance, but re-verify the exact string before putting it in live creative. ---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — the 6 things that matter most

1. **The product is identity, not erections.** Across every source, the core wound is *"I feel less of a man."* Sell restored masculinity / confidence / "feel like myself again" — the mechanical fix is the *feature*, the reclaimed identity is the *desire*.
2. **Two markets, two playbooks.** ED is a **Schwartz Stage 4–5** (saturated, ~30 yrs post-Viagra) market → win on **identity, trust, discretion, and a *fresh* mechanism** (young-men / porn-induced / anxiety). PE is **Stage 3–4** (less saturated, no "Viagra moment") → you can still **lead with a unique mechanism + proof numbers** ("from 30 seconds to X minutes").
3. **The young-men surge is the most under-served angle.** ED in men under 40 is now **up to ~30%**, yet only **~14–28%** ever seek help. Huge **problem-aware-but-not-acting** pool, emotionally raw, self-blaming, and *least* targeted by legacy "old guy with heart disease" Viagra positioning.
4. **Urgency is ~8.5/10 but shame-throttled.** Desire intensity is near-10; action is suppressed by embarrassment. **Discretion + "no awkward doctor visit" is the friction-remover that unlocks the buy** (the entire Hims/Ro/BlueChew wedge).
5. **The default buyer mindset is skeptical and disappointed, not curious.** They've typically tried **3–5 prior solutions** ("feels like a placebo," "I've tried everything"). You must defeat *"this is probably a scam"* with concrete proof up front.
6. **The #1 reputational landmine in the category is the subscription/billing trap.** "Charged after I cancelled," "no refunds" — transparent cancellation + honest money-back is a genuine competitive moat, not a nicety.

# 1. AUDIENCE IDENTIFICATION

## Age & gender
**Buyer = the male sufferer. Female partner = powerful influencer + secondary buyer/researcher.**

**Erectile Dysfunction — prevalence by age:**

| Age bracket | Prevalence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 18–24 | ~17.9% meet ED criteria | 2021 US Nat. Survey of Sexual Wellbeing |
| 18–34 | **26.0%** (IIEF-5); only **2.6%** currently treated | 2021 US survey |
| 18–35 (porn study) | **21.5%** had some degree of ED | Jacobs et al. 2021 |
| 40 | ~22–40% | MMAS |
| 40–70 (overall) | **52%** total; complete impotence 5%→15% across 40→70 | Massachusetts Male Aging Study |
| 70 | ~49% | MMAS-aligned |

> According to PubMed, ED prevalence in young men has been estimated **as high as 30%**, and the share of under-40s among new ED patients **tripled** (~5%→>15%) at one clinic over five years. Psychogenic causes (anxiety, depression, partner difficulty) dominate the young cohort. [Nguyen et al., *Sex Med Rev* 2017, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sxmr.2017.05.004)] · [Jacobs et al., *JMIR Public Health* 2021 — 21.5% of men 18–35, linked to problematic porn use, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2196/32542)]

**Premature Ejaculation — prevalence:**
- **Most common male sexual dysfunction**, ~**20–30%** of men (US PEPA survey: 24%; NHSLS: 31% of men 18–59).
- **Not strongly age-dependent** → PE buyer skews **younger and more digitally native** than the classic ED buyer.

**Critical funnel insight:** Only **~14% of men 18–34** have ever discussed ED with a provider (vs ~55% of men 65+). According to PubMed, a study scraping r/ErectileDysfunction found the **median age of posters was 24**, and **only 28%** had seen a healthcare professional. The vocal online market is *young, untreated, and self-diagnosing.* [Jiang et al., *Urology* 2020, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.urology.2020.04.100)]

**The partner as second buyer:** Female partners experience rejection and **self-blame** — *"Is it me?" / "Maybe he's not attracted to me."* ~1 in 7 women report being *blamed* by their partner for his ED. In PE, **53% of men feel they're "letting their partner down."** The partner is both a *motivation lever* ("stop letting her wonder if it's her fault") and a *purchase trigger.*

| Unaware | ~10% | ~20% | Blame "a bad night," alcohol, the partner; haven't named a condition (higher for PE). |

**Strategic read:** the money is in converting the **problem-aware** pool — especially young men who feel broken but believe nothing will help. **Lead with empathy + a credible mechanism, not a feature list.**

## Market sophistication (Schwartz 1–5)
- **ED = Stage 4–5 (saturated).** Viagra launched **1998**; generic sildenafil, Cialis, telehealth giants, chewables/dissolvables. US ED-drug market **$1.36B (2024) → ~$2.16B by 2030**. Bare benefit claims ("get hard again") are exhausted → win via **(a) a fresh believable mechanism** (the young-men/PIED/anxiety angle reframes ED as *psychological & reversible*) or **(b) Stage-5 identification** (brand, community, discretion, "guys like you").
- **PE = Stage 3–4 (maturing).** No blockbuster brand moment; fragmented treatments; many men think it's untreatable. **You can still lead with a unique mechanism + concrete proof numbers.**

## Emotional state (with evidence)
The arc to sell along: **Shame/"broken" → Skepticism ("tried everything") → Hopelessness/avoidance → Hope via a credible reason ("it's not you, it's [mechanism], and it's reversible").**

- **Desperate / event-driven:** *"I have an important date in a few days. I can't get an erection. It started a day ago when I was thinking about erectile dysfunction. How do I get an erection again?"* [Quora, indexed]
- **Ashamed / "broken":** a real Patient.info thread is titled word-for-word *"Erectile Dysfunction; 25, Healthy, Lean, Sexually Broken."*
- **"Less of a man":** *"I felt less of a man when the problem started"* and *"I was dying inside for a while."* [Irish Times, VERBATIM first-person feature]
- **Hopeless/avoidant:** clinically, ED men are ~2× as likely to develop depression; one Reddit-scrape study found **38% reported depressive symptoms, 2% mentioned self-harm/suicidality.** [Jiang 2020]
- **Skeptical:** *"No results after one week. Feels as if I were taking a placebo!"* [Trustpilot/BlueChew, indexed]

# 2. PAIN & PROBLEMS

## Core pain point
**The collapse of masculine identity — he no longer feels like a man.** ED/PE is experienced not as a medical malfunction but as a *verdict on his worth*. Three fused layers: (1) *I am defective as a male*, (2) *I can't do the one thing a partner needs*, (3) *she'll find out and leave/replace me.*

> **[VERBATIM, PE focus group]** *"When you can't satisfy your woman, you somehow feel like there's a large part of you that is missing or failed."* [Revicki et al., *Health Qual Life Outcomes* 2008, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/1477-7525-6-33)]

## Symptoms of the pain point (18)
**Physical:**
1. Can't achieve an erection at all (esp. first time with a new partner)
2. Can't *maintain* it — loses it partway through ("going soft" / "losing wood")
3. Finishing in under a minute / 30 seconds–2 minutes (PE)
4. **No morning wood / loss of spontaneous erections** (the #1 self-diagnostic men obsess over)
5. Weak or "half" erections insufficient for penetration
6. Loss of libido even when he *wants* to want it
7. Genital numbness / weak, pleasureless orgasm (esp. SSRI/PSSD cases)
8. Delayed ejaculation / can't finish at all (the opposite failure)
9. Can only get hard to porn, not to a real partner (PIED)

**Emotional/behavioral:**
10. Performance-anxiety spiral / "spectatoring" (watching himself fail in real time)
11. Avoiding sex entirely
12. Avoiding dating altogether
13. Pulling away from the partner to pre-empt failure
14. Depression / despair ("slides into despair, fear and hopelessness")
15. Low self-esteem / feeling "useless" / "worthless"
16. Obsessive rumination (problem starts the moment he *thinks* about it)
17. Faking tiredness / making excuses to dodge sex
18. Using alcohol as a crutch (which makes it worse)

## The unique mechanism (why they keep struggling)
**A self-fulfilling anxiety loop with a physiological engine — this is the single most powerful reframe in the category, because it relocates the "failure" from his character to his nervous system.**

> One failure → fear of failing again → anticipatory anxiety before the next encounter → the body reads anxiety as danger → **fight-or-flight floods adrenaline & cortisol → blood flow diverts AWAY from the penis** → erection fails (or arousal over-spikes → premature ejaculation) → "proof" he's broken → deeper anxiety.

**[VERBATIM, young-ED patient]** the overthinking that drives the loop: *"My brain goes into overdrive, going through all kinds of possible situations; what did I say? When will we see each other next time? Will it be possible to have sex then?"* (Participant 12) [Grénman/Lundgren et al., *Scand J Prim Health Care* 2024, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2024.2327501)]

**Secondary root causes feeding the loop:**
- **Porn-induced ED (PIED):** internet porn = supernormal stimulus → repeated dopamine floods → receptor desensitization → real partners "may no longer be sufficient to trigger a full erectile response."
- **Vascular / blood-flow** and **low testosterone**.
- **SSRIs / PSSD:** antidepressants cause sexual dysfunction in a large share of users; post-SSRI dysfunction (genital numbness, low libido, ED) can persist after stopping.
- **The trap:** every "solution attempt" (trying harder, self-monitoring, watching porn to "warm up," secretly taking a pill and panicking) *feeds* the anxiety that causes the failure.

## Daily frustrations (18)
1. **Dread building for days before a date**
2. The **"when do I tell a new partner?"** agony (a real forum thread is literally titled *"When do I tell new partners about erectile dysfunction?"*)
3. The catastrophic **first-time-with-someone-new** failure
4. **Pill timing logistics** — having to swallow Viagra "an hour or two before" kills spontaneity (men switch to daily Cialis just to feel normal)
5. Lying / making excuses to avoid initiating
6. Watching the partner's face fall in real time
7. Comparing himself to porn performers / "what she's used to"
8. Worrying mid-sex whether the erection will hold instead of being present
9. Partner going quiet / pulling away (read as confirmation)
10. The **morning no-morning-wood check** that sets his whole day's mood
11. Needing porn to "get started" even with a willing partner present
12. Finishing before she's even aroused, and the silent aftermath
13. The doctor-visit dread (fear it's something permanent)
14. Being told *"it's just anxiety / in your head"* and feeling dismissed
15. Hiding pills from a partner / fear of being caught
16. Declining new relationships to avoid the situation
17. Replaying the last failure on a loop before the next attempt
18. **[VERBATIM]** retreating to masturbation because it's "safe": *"Instead of seeking sex [with the partner], I have masturbated because I can manage that. But it seemed pretty unfair."* (Participant 5) [Grénman 2024]

## What they're embarrassed about
- **Saying it out loud at all** ("Let's talk about ED — said no dude ever")
- **Raising it with a doctor** (documented #1 barrier; men "put it off as long as they can")
- Being seen buying/taking pills — "the guy on Viagra at 28"
- **Finishing in seconds** — feeling "inadequate," "disappointing"
- Admitting it's **porn-related** (a layer of shame on top of the shame)
- **Asking for help** — admitting weakness violates the masculine script
- **[VERBATIM]** *"It's generally not something I talk about"; "I needed a little bit of courage to come in."* [Revicki 2008]

## Specific fears (12)
1. **She'll leave me** (documented: a real fiancée broke off her engagement over it). **[VERBATIM]** *"...my girlfriend says... this relationship is over if things don't improve."* [Revicki 2008]
2. **She'll cheat / find someone who "works"** (some partners in the study did report seeking satisfaction elsewhere)
3. **It's permanent — I'll never be normal again**
4. **It's a sign of serious disease** (heart/vascular) — men seek doctors "to exclude physical illness"
5. **I'll be laughed at / humiliated** if it gets out
6. **Infertility / I can't give her a child** — *"since I have only managed twice in all these years... you have to do it some other way to be able to have a child."* (Participant 13) [Grénman 2024]
7. **I've permanently broken my brain with porn**
8. **The pills will stop working / I'll be dependent forever** (young men specifically fear *"becoming dependent on medications"* and that needing pills young *"made them feel like a failure"*) [Grénman 2024]
9. **I'll fail again next time** (the anticipatory fear that drives the loop)
10. **No one will ever want to date me** if they know
11. **She's only staying out of pity**
12. **The SSRI damage is irreversible** (PSSD dread)

## What they're angry about — and who they blame
*(Each blame-target is a distinct message angle.)*
- **Themselves** — *"I did this to myself"* (porn, lifestyle). The most corrosive, default target.
- **Porn / the porn industry** — feeling "robbed," conditioned before they ever had real sex.
- **SSRIs & the doctors who prescribed them without warning** — the sharpest anger; a sense of *betrayal* ("no one told me this could happen").
- **Dismissive doctors** — *"it's in your head," "you're too young for this."* One man was told his PE *"would disappear once he got married."* [Grénman 2024]
- **"Big Pharma"** — for selling symptom-maskers (and dependence/cost) rather than a cure.
- **Society / the masculinity script** — anger at the unspoken rule that a man's worth = his erection.
- **Partners** — an uglier strand: deflecting the wound outward (the ~1-in-7 "male blame" finding).

# 3. DESIRES

## Primary desire (ranked)
1. **Reclaiming masculinity / "feeling like a man again"** — the deepest driver, cuts across ED *and* PE.
2. **Confidence** — the socially-safe word men use to *name* desire #1 out loud. The single most-repeated word in the dataset.
3. **Pleasing the partner / not letting her down** — strongest in PE.
4. **Intimacy / connection (fear of losing the relationship).** **[VERBATIM]** *"It definitely takes the intimacy out, and it just becomes physical at that point"; "I'm looking for the intimacy that we lost."* [Revicki 2008]
5. **Control** (PE-specific, very literal). **[VERBATIM]** *"I feel if I had control, then this wouldn't be an issue at all"; "the ultimate success would be unlimited control."* [Revicki 2008]
6. **Spontaneity / "feeling normal/young again"** (post-solution) — *"You gave me my life back."*
7. **Status** — least directly voiced; encoded in the *terror of the labels* ("one-pump chump," "minute man," "whiskey dick").

> **Positioning takeaway:** sell *"feel like a man / get your confidence back / be ready anytime,"* not *"treat erectile dysfunction."*

## Urgency: **8.5 / 10**
- Acute, identity-level, *compounding* pain (each failure deepens the loop).
- Event-driven spikes → impulse buying ("important date in a few days").
- Relationship-threat raises stakes to "lose my partner."
- **Not a 10** because **shame throttles fast action** — men stew for months/years. → **Discretion + privacy + "no awkward appointment" is the lever that converts latent urgency into a purchase.**

## Emotional triggers by desire
- **Masculinity →** shame, humiliation, "less of a man," "old before my time," "failure as a man."
- **Confidence →** anticipatory dread, the "in my head" spiral, avoidance, withdrawal from dating.
- **Pleasing partner →** guilt, "letting her down," fear she's faking, fear of comparison.
- **Intimacy/loss →** fear of abandonment, "she thinks I'm not attracted to her," resentment, distance.
- **Control (PE) →** frustration, embarrassment at the *speed*, "here we go again."
- **Status →** fear of the labels, fear of buddies/partner finding out.
- **Spontaneity (solved) →** relief, pride, reclaimed youth, "wished I'd done it sooner."

## Their ideal day (solved problem, in their voice)
*"I wake up with wood like I'm 18 again, I don't think about it anymore, when it happens it just happens, I'm rock hard, I last as long as I want, she finishes, and I feel like a man again."*
Components: morning wood is back (= "everything works") · no planning, no clock (sex when the moment's right) · rock-hard on demand, zero anxiety in his head · lasts long enough to focus on *her* · sees her satisfied (the trophy) · pride + reclaimed identity.

## How they measure success
- **Time on the clock (PE):** from "<1 minute" to a target ("5–10 minutes," aspirational "an hour")
- **Hardness:** "rock hard," self-rated % ("75–80%" → "fully hard")
- **Morning wood returning** = the #1 "I'm fixed" signal
- **Spontaneity / no planning** ("not catching the train")
- **"Out of my head" / no anxiety** during sex
- **Partner's reaction** — her orgasm / visible satisfaction / reassurance
- **Staying hard the *entire* time** (not going soft mid-act)
- **Confidence restored** — the umbrella metric: "feel like the man I used to be"

- **Delay sprays / numbing:** *"the whole time it felt as though my penis had been removed... I just could NOT orgasm"* · *"left you numb, you cannot feel anything... cannot enjoy anything"* · numbing condoms can *worsen* erections and cause **partner numbness** (solving PE triggers ED)
- **SSRIs:** anorgasmia / ED — trading one dysfunction for another
- **Dapoxetine/Priligy:** nausea (up to 22%), dizziness, **fainting/syncope** (worse with alcohol)
- **Devices:** priapism risk if a ring is left on >30 min; pump bruising/tissue damage
- **Supplements:** *"tasted awful and did nothing"*; documented scams (ExtenZe lawsuit, FDA warnings)
- **Telehealth horror cluster (the biggest one):** *"didn't work... feels like a placebo,"* then **"charged after I cancelled," "no refunds possible," "impossible to cancel."** Hims sits ~3.0–3.4/5 on Trustpilot, dominated by billing complaints.

## Number of solutions tried before seeking a new one
- **PE: ~3–5** (a behavioral technique + a spray/condom + a pill or SSRI, often + a supplement)
- **ED: ~2–4** (often a supplement first, then Viagra, then Cialis/telehealth)
- → The new buyer is **skeptical and disappointed by default** ("I've tried everything," "feels like a placebo"), *not* curious.

# 7. OBJECTIONS

## Likely objections (ranked)
1. **"Does it actually work, or is it a placebo/scam?"** (highest priority — counter with mechanism + concrete proof numbers)
2. **"I've tried everything and nothing worked."** (differentiate on *mechanism*, not claims)
3. **"Will it numb me / my partner — will we lose all sensation?"** (defining PE/topical objection)
4. **"Will it ruin spontaneity — do I have to plan and time it?"**
5. **"Is it safe / what are the side effects?"** (headaches, vision, fainting, priapism)
6. **"Is this a subscription trap — charged forever, can't cancel?"** (as strong as efficacy doubts)
7. **"Too expensive for something that might not work."**
8. **"Embarrassed to buy — will the package be discreet?"**
9. **"Do I need a prescription / a doctor visit?"**

## Pre-purchase questions
- How fast does it work?
- How long does it last?
- Will it specifically make me **last longer** (IELT), not just get hard?
- Do I need a prescription / doctor visit?
- What are the side effects / is it safe long-term?
- **Can I drink alcohol with it?** (very common)
- Will my partner notice / will it transfer to her?
- Is it natural or is it drugs?
- Is shipping discreet?
- Is there a money-back guarantee / how do I cancel?

# STRATEGIC READ FOR THE OFFER
The category's deepest wounds: (1) **numbness destroying pleasure for him AND her**, (2) **pills/SSRIs trading one dysfunction for another**, (3) **"planning sex" killing intimacy**, (4) **telehealth billing betrayal after the product didn't even work**. A winning new product positions against all four — *works without numbing · preserves spontaneity · no nasty side effects · no subscription trap / honest money-back* — and **leads with empathy + a fresh, believable mechanism** ("it's not you, it's [X], and it's reversible") to defeat the default *"this is probably a scam"* mindset of a buyer who's already failed with 3–5 prior solutions, and to reach the huge, under-served, problem-aware pool of **young men who feel broken but don't yet believe anything will help.**
